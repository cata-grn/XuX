SEED FUNDING TARGET: £1,050,000 via Ordinary Shares

1. NATURE OF THE TRADE AND INNOVATION
XuX Global Technologies Ltd is a high-growth UK technology company developing 
a proprietary, serverless, polymorphic software infrastructure. The system is 
engineered from scratch to provide post-quantum cryptographic protection and 
decentralized multi-currency transaction rails across 26 industrial sectors. 
The core innovation uses a time-stamped mutation vector that dynamically alters 
internal software processes every single millisecond, making it immune to 
DDoS/Flood attacks and external breaches.

2. USE OF SEED CAPITAL (GROWTH AND DEVELOPMENT)
All funds raised from private UK Angel Investors (£1,050,000) combined with 
leveraged non-dilutive UK Government Grants (£2,450,000) will be spent strictly 
on carrying out a qualifying trade. 100% of the capital expenditure is 
allocated to research and development (R&D) infrastructure, multi-region cloud 
serverless node networks, national military-grade security audits, and global 
intellectual property patenting.

3. RISK-TO-CAPITAL COMPLIANCE REVIEW
The company meets the statutory risk-to-capital requirements. The investment 
carries a genuine risk of capital loss, as it involves pioneering non-open-source 
post-quantum cryptography software developed exclusively by the solo founder. 
There are no pre-arranged exit strategies or capital protection mechanisms for 
the investors, ensuring complete alignment with HMRC guidelines.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY SECURITY
The core software stack and all digital currency protocols are licensed to the 
UK company under a strict technology assignment framework owned by the founder, 
R.C.F. This maximizes corporate asset value while ensuring long-term operational 
stability within the United Kingdom.
